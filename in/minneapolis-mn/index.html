EV fast chargers near food in Minneapolis, MN

122 DC fast chargers sit within 25 miles of downtown Minneapolis, across every network, and 82 of them have somewhere to eat within a 10-minute walk, so the charging stop and the meal are the same stop.

Typical power here is 120 kW, and 52 of the 122 charge at 150 kW or more. 55 of the stops with food are listed as open 24 hours. Most are on Dirt Road (27), ChargePoint (19), Tesla Supercharger (15).
