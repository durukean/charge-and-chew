The Road Food Index

How often is there somewhere to eat within a 10-minute walk of a US fast charger, and which chains, interstates and states do best? We matched all 13,926 public DC fast-charger sites in the US Department of Energy’s database against 90 restaurant and store chains mapped in OpenStreetMap. Data as of November 11, 2026.

Key findings

  1. 77.5% of US DC fast-charger sites (10,797 of 13,926) have somewhere to eat within a 10-minute walk.
  2. McDonald's is within a 10-minute walk of more fast chargers than any other restaurant chain: 4,649 sites, 33.4% of all of them, just ahead of Starbucks (4,552).
  3. Of the restaurant chains, In-N-Out has a fast charger within a walk of the most of its locations: 52.9% of 431. Among all chains it is Flying J, at 75.7% of 218.
  4. I-5 is the best interstate for food at a fast charger: 90.4% of its chargers have somewhere to eat within a walk. I-91 is the worst, at 63.6%.
  5. On the 152 drives we plan, the longest stretch with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the road is 144 miles, between Lusk, WY and Rapid City, SD (Denver to Rapid City).

How we counted

  • Chargers: every public DC fast-charger site in the US DOE / NREL Alternative Fuels Data Center station list, all networks, as the map shows them: under 24 kW, private and truck-only sites left out, and one site per location however many stalls it lists. 13,926 sites.
  • Chains: restaurant and store locations from OpenStreetMap, by brand. Map entries of one chain within 250 m of each other count as one location (a store and its fuel station are often mapped separately). OpenStreetMap is volunteer-made and misses some locations.
  • Within a walk: within 800 m in a straight line, about 10 minutes at 3 mph. Walk times are minimums; the real walk is usually longer. “Somewhere to eat” is any of the 50 restaurant chains we track or an ice cream shop.
  • Interstates: the road as OpenStreetMap maps it; miles are measured along the line the map draws, which merges both directions and can differ a little from official lengths.
  • Drives: the 152 routes of our road trip guides, as the app plans them.

Data as of November 11, 2026. It is refreshed weekly, so the numbers on this page move with it.
