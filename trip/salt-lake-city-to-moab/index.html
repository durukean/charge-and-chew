Questions about this drive

The break in Price, UT at 10:20 am has KFC 1 min from the charger. In all, 67 of the roughly 80 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Salt Lake City and Moab?

About 80 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 234-mile route, 77 of them 50 kW or more. 44 charge at 150 kW or more, 19 are on the Tesla network, and 67 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Salt Lake City and Moab?

66 miles, between mile 52 (Spanish Fork, UT) and mile 118 (Price, UT), with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Salt Lake City to Moab?

How long does it take to charge on the way from Salt Lake City to Moab?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 23 min for a Tesla Model Y at the 300 kW charger in Price, UT; about 45 min for a Ford Mustang Mach-E at the 300 kW charger in Green River, UT; about 25 min for a Hyundai Ioniq 5 at the 300 kW charger in Green River, UT. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
