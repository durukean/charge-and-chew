Questions about this drive

Where should I stop to eat between Phoenix and Sedona?

Leaving Phoenix at 8:00 am you arrive around 10:15 am, too soon for a meal stop. About halfway, Mayer, AZ (mile 65) has Subway a 1-minute walk from a 150 kW charger. In all, 27 of the 28 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Phoenix and Sedona?

28 DC fast chargers of 50 kW or more are within 5 miles of the 116-mile route. 20 of them charge at 150 kW or more, 11 are on the Tesla network, and 27 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Phoenix and Sedona?

30 miles, between mile 35 (New River, AZ) and mile 65 (Mayer, AZ), with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Phoenix to Sedona?

Counting the fast chargers of 50 kW or more on the route, the chains most often within a 10-minute walk are McDonald's (near 12 chargers), Subway (12), Starbucks (10), Taco Bell (9), Chipotle (6) and Jack in the Box (6).

How long does it take to charge on the way from Phoenix to Sedona?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 38 min for a Tesla Model Y at the 150 kW charger in Mayer, AZ. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
