Questions about this drive

Where should I stop to eat between Denver and Rapid City?

Leaving Denver at 8:00 am, lunch falls in Lusk, WY around 12:55 pm, where Subway is a 3-minute walk from a 120 kW charger. The break in Cheyenne, WY at 9:45 am has Denny's 1 min from the charger. In all, 66 of the roughly 83 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Denver and Rapid City?

About 83 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 406-mile route, 77 of them 50 kW or more. 43 charge at 150 kW or more, 13 are on the Tesla network, and 66 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Denver and Rapid City?

144 miles, between mile 257 (Lusk, WY) and mile 401 (Rapid City, SD), with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Denver to Rapid City?

Counting the fast chargers of 50 kW or more on the route, the chains most often within a 10-minute walk are Starbucks (near 40 chargers), McDonald's (28), Subway (23), Taco Bell (19), Wendy's (19) and Burger King (12).

How long does it take to charge on the way from Denver to Rapid City?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 48 min for a Tesla Model Y at the 120 kW charger in Lusk, WY; about 45 min for a Ford Mustang Mach-E at the 350 kW charger in Cheyenne, WY; about 25 min for a Hyundai Ioniq 5 at the 350 kW charger in Cheyenne, WY. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
