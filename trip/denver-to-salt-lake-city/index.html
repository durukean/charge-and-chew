Questions about this drive

How many fast chargers are there between Denver and Salt Lake City?

About 114 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 519-mile route, 109 of them 50 kW or more. 65 charge at 150 kW or more, 18 are on the Tesla network, and 95 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Denver and Salt Lake City?

98 miles, between mile 341 (Rock Springs, WY) and mile 439 (Evanston, WY), with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Denver to Salt Lake City?

Counting the fast chargers of 50 kW or more on the route, the chains most often within a 10-minute walk are McDonald's (near 46 chargers), Starbucks (45), Subway (34), Wendy's (28), Taco Bell (22) and Burger King (21).

How long does it take to charge on the way from Denver to Salt Lake City?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 38 min for a Tesla Model Y at the 150 kW charger in Laramie, WY; about 45 min for a Ford Mustang Mach-E at the 350 kW charger in Rawlins, WY; about 25 min for a Hyundai Ioniq 5 at the 350 kW charger in Rawlins, WY. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
