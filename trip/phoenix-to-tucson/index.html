Questions about this drive

Where should I stop to eat between Phoenix and Tucson?

Leaving Phoenix at 8:00 am you arrive around 10:00 am, too soon for a meal stop. About halfway, Eloy, AZ (mile 54) has Burger King a 2-minute walk from a 400 kW charger. In all, 41 of the roughly 43 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Phoenix and Tucson?

About 43 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 113-mile route, 43 of them 50 kW or more. 28 charge at 150 kW or more, 9 are on the Tesla network, and 41 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Phoenix and Tucson?

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Phoenix to Tucson?

How long does it take to charge on the way from Phoenix to Tucson?

It depends on the car. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
