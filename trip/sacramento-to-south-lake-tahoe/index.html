Questions about this drive

Where should I stop to eat between Sacramento and South Lake Tahoe?

Leaving Sacramento at 8:00 am you arrive around 10:15 am, too soon for a meal stop. About halfway, Placerville, CA (mile 43) has Jack in the Box a 1-minute walk from a 400 kW charger. In all, 88 of the roughly 96 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Sacramento and South Lake Tahoe?

About 96 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 104-mile route, 94 of them 50 kW or more. 62 charge at 150 kW or more, 25 are on the Tesla network, and 88 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Sacramento and South Lake Tahoe?

35 miles, between mile 62 (Pollock Pines, CA) and mile 97 (South Lake Tahoe, CA), with no DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Sacramento to South Lake Tahoe?

Counting the fast chargers of 50 kW or more on the route, the chains most often within a 10-minute walk are Starbucks (near 62 chargers), McDonald's (35), Subway (31), Taco Bell (28), Chipotle (27) and Panda Express (22).

How long does it take to charge on the way from Sacramento to South Lake Tahoe?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 23 min for a Tesla Model Y at the 400 kW charger in Placerville, CA; about 45 min for a Ford Mustang Mach-E at the 400 kW charger in Placerville, CA; about 25 min for a Hyundai Ioniq 5 at the 400 kW charger in Placerville, CA. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
