Questions about this drive

Where should I stop to eat between Denver and Colorado Springs?

Leaving Denver at 8:00 am you arrive around 9:20 am, too soon for a meal stop. About halfway, Castle Rock, CO (mile 24) has Del Taco a 1-minute walk from a 350 kW charger. In all, 65 of the roughly 80 fast chargers along the way have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

How many fast chargers are there between Denver and Colorado Springs?

About 80 DC fast chargers are within 5 miles of the 68-mile route, 78 of them 50 kW or more. 46 charge at 150 kW or more, 14 are on the Tesla network, and 65 have a restaurant within a 10-minute walk.

What is the longest stretch without a fast charger between Denver and Colorado Springs?

No stretch is longer than 18 miles: there is a DC fast charger within 5 miles of the route at least that often.

Which restaurant chains are on the way from Denver to Colorado Springs?

Counting the fast chargers of 50 kW or more on the route, the chains most often within a 10-minute walk are Starbucks (near 37 chargers), McDonald's (27), Chick-fil-A (20), Chipotle (20), Subway (19) and Taco Bell (16).

How long does it take to charge on the way from Denver to Colorado Springs?

It depends on the car. The app's estimate for a 10–80% charge is about 45 min for a Ford Mustang Mach-E at the 350 kW charger in Castle Rock, CO; about 25 min for a Hyundai Ioniq 5 at the 350 kW charger in Castle Rock, CO. These are rough figures from each car's battery size and peak charging speed; a cold battery or a busy site takes longer.
