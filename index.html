Charge & Chew

13,000+ public DC fast chargers across the US — Tesla, Electrify America, EVgo, ChargePoint, IONNA, Rivian and more (from the US DOE database) — matched against the restaurants and stores within a 10-minute walk, so you can plan a stop around what you'll actually do while the car charges.

How to use it: set your car for connector compatibility and charge-time estimates, tap chains to filter, search a place or drop a pin to explore an area, or plan a route to see every matching stop along the way. Tap ⭐ to save a stop.

Chargers are colour-coded by network on the map (Tesla red, Electrify America green, EVgo teal, ChargePoint orange…). We drop listings under 24 kW, heavy-truck-only depots, duplicates and anything marked private, and we flag dealership lots because access and hours are often restricted.

Charger data from the US DOE / NREL Alternative Fuel Stations database; chain locations from OpenStreetMap. Walk times are straight-line estimates at ~3 mph and charge times are rough estimates — always verify before relying on them. Coverage and pricing come from public data and can be out of date.

Isolated stops: surveys consistently find most EV drivers will detour for a charger that isn't in an empty, unlit lot. We flag stops with nothing we track within a 10-minute walk so you can judge — it's a proxy from our own data, not a safety rating.

Compatibility notes: "needs adapter" means your car can physically connect with a common adapter — for Tesla Superchargers your car's brand must also be allowed on that site (check the Tesla app). Charge times are rough 10–80% estimates; walk times are minimums based on straight-line distance.
