Try searching Starbucks · tacos in Denver · LA to Las Vegas with In-N-Out · I-5 · ice cream · bakery (kinds of place we don't track are looked up live for the area you're viewing). Tap ☆ on a stop to save it.

On the map each big network has its own mark — Tesla's red T, ChargePoint's C, Electrify America's EA, EVgo's e — and smaller networks share a plain dot. They're our own marks, not the networks' logos.

Good to know

  • Walk times are straight-line minimums at ~3 mph; charge times are rough 10–80% estimates.
  • Needs adapter: your car connects with a common adapter. At a Tesla Supercharger your brand must also be allowed — check the Tesla app.
  • Isolated: nothing we track within a 10-minute walk. Worth a thought before a night charge.
  • Open and closed come from posted hours where OpenStreetMap has them; on a route they're for when you'd arrive.
  • No live availability or prices — check the network's own app before you rely on a stop.
