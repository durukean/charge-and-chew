Charge & Chew

Every Tesla Supercharger in the US, matched against the restaurants and stores within a 10-minute walk — so you can plan a stop around what you actually want to do while the car charges.

How to use it: tap chains to filter, search a place or drop a 📌 pin to explore an area, or hit 🛣️ to plan a route and see every matching stop along the way. Tap ⭐ to save a stop for later.

Charger data from supercharge.info; chain locations from OpenStreetMap. Walk times are straight-line estimates at ~3 mph — always check the real route. Chain coverage runs about 90–95%, so a missing chain doesn't guarantee it isn't there.
