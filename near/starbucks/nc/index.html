How many EV chargers have a Starbucks within walking distance in North Carolina?

77 public DC fast chargers in North Carolina have a Starbucks within a 10-minute walk (800 m), based on US Department of Energy charger data matched against OpenStreetMap locations.

Which Starbucks charging stop is fastest in North Carolina?

Can I charge a non-Tesla EV at these stops?

Most are CCS or have CCS alongside NACS. Set your car in the map view and it will show connector compatibility and an estimated charge time for each stop. Tesla Supercharger sites additionally have to be open to your brand.

How accurate are the walk times?
