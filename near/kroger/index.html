Common questions

How many EV chargers have a Kroger within walking distance in the US?

259 public DC fast chargers in the US have a Kroger within a 10-minute walk (800 m), based on US Department of Energy charger data matched against OpenStreetMap locations.

Which Kroger charging stop is fastest in the US?

Athens North MB.HPC in Athens at 400 kW.

Can I charge a non-Tesla EV at these stops?

Most are CCS or have CCS alongside NACS. Set your car in the map view and it will show connector compatibility and an estimated charge time for each stop. Tesla Supercharger sites additionally have to be open to your brand.

How accurate are the walk times?

They are minimums: straight-line distance at about 3 mph. The real walk is usually longer because you have to follow paths and crossings.
