How many EV chargers have a Jack in the Box within walking distance in California?

521 public DC fast chargers in California have a Jack in the Box within a 10-minute walk (800 m), based on US Department of Energy charger data matched against OpenStreetMap locations.

Which Jack in the Box charging stop is fastest in California?

Can I charge a non-Tesla EV at these stops?

Most are CCS or have CCS alongside NACS. Set your car in the map view and it will show connector compatibility and an estimated charge time for each stop. Tesla Supercharger sites additionally have to be open to your brand.

How accurate are the walk times?
